r.sim.water improvements

SIMWE overland flow and sediment transport, corrected for parallel runs

  • Broken nblock auto-split disabled; flagged as errata #7358
  • Parallel runs no longer skip walkers #7964
  • Water depth accumulated atomically; parallel runs had been losing 12–14% of water #7974
  • New pytest coverage with synthetic data; per-walker random numbers make results order-independent

by Václav Petráš and Anna Petrášová · shipping in GRASS 8.6

SIMWE correctness work for 8.6 by Vaclav Petras and Anna Petrasova: Anna's PR 7358 disabled the broken nblock split (May 2026, errata); PR 7964 fixed parallel runs skipping walkers; PR 7974 made depth accumulation atomic so parallel runs stop losing 12 to 14 percent of water; PR 7980 gave each walker its own random numbers, so results no longer depend on thread order. Two tutorials show the full chain: Anna's parallel subwatershed workflow (Sep 2025) and my soil-informed infiltration tutorial with SSURGO and ROSETTA (merged Oct 22, 2026).
